Obol/libBObol Design Review and Recommended Improvements
========================================================

Purpose
-------

This document records the recommended cleanup and hardening work following the
initial performance work on Obol, libBObol, and their BRL-CAD clients.

The present architecture is viable and should not be rewritten from scratch.
Its central contracts and performance mechanisms are sound.  The principal
design debt is concentrated ownership: several very large implementation units
now combine state machines, realization, presentation, policy, and client
integration.  Those boundaries should be made explicit without undoing the
large-scene performance behavior already achieved.

Intermediate API and implementation compatibility is not a goal.  User-facing
behavior, correctness, maintainability, and large-model performance are the
goals.


Current review status (2026-08-09)
----------------------------------

The architecture is now beyond several items in the original roadmap below:

* the allocation-free LoD phase machine, typed epoch/routing domains, canonical
  result payload category, process-wide bounded source-realization service,
  renderer configuration snapshot, qged diagnostics unit, and interruptible
  renderer executors exist;
* source realization no longer joins a per-draw worker during ordinary handle
  teardown;
* both System GL and OSMesa pass the Generic Twin cold/warm shaded/wire smoke
  matrix with stable convergence, no coordinator invariant violations, and no
  terminal structural boxes; and
* host/event presentation cadence has been removed from LoD capacity feedback.
  CPU traversal time, asynchronous GPU timing, and deadline overruns are the
  actionable capacity signals.  This prevents a slow mouse or scripted event
  stream from coarsening a retained cut which the renderer draws quickly.
* lighting now has one controller-owned policy with named Studio and historical
  MGED profiles.  Studio is the default, both profiles feed GL and retained RT,
  and qged exposes the choice through `view lighting profile` and the typed
  display-endpoint property;
* System GL shaders and OSMesa fixed-function rendering consume the same
  explicit ambient, material, and complete light snapshot.  The OSMesa batch
  path disables `GL_COLOR_MATERIAL` while installing CAD materials and clears
  unused light slots, eliminating backend brightness drift and stale
  Studio-to-MGED lights; and
* compound graphical-test framing is now atomic.  Orientation plus autoview is
  applied with Qt updates suppressed until the final camera is valid, removing
  a harness-generated one-frame view jump without changing production camera
  or LoD behavior.

Unified resource feedback is now complete for the current renderer tiers.
Obol publishes allocation, pressure, eviction, reclamation, and upload
snapshots; libBObol samples each unique SoCADAssembly once per completed frame
and retains an O(1) aggregate for controller and HUD consumers.  CPU and GPU
pressure are explicit coordinator inputs.  A pressure revision requests one
coverage- and interaction-safe compaction pass; unavoidable persistent
pressure becomes a stable memory-limited state instead of a recovery loop.
Perf sampling of the final Generic Twin cold/warm replay placed the aggregate
refresh at 0.01 percent or below, with framebuffer PNG compression—not LoD
resource accounting—dominating the instrumented harness.

The remaining major design gaps, in priority order, are:

1. Coordinator authority is incomplete.  The explicit state machine now
   canonicalizes contradictory observations and owns named phase transitions,
   and allocation-free coordinator policies now own completed-frame resource
   pressure edges/recovery revisions and the one-shot late calibrated-headroom
   witness.  A third value policy owns resident-compaction requests, quiet
   deadlines, visual-settling admission, partial planning continuation, and
   demand revisions.  A fourth owns result-publication batches, adaptive
   deadlines, and the unique requested-frame witness which replaces each
   batch timer.  A fifth owns minimum-mesh coverage accumulation across
   bounded compact-index windows, rescan-safe completion, missing-coverage
   classification, and the authoritative visible denominator.  Camera and
   inventory epochs invalidate that proof while quality-only passes may retain
   it without a whole-hierarchy recount.  A sixth policy owns the public
   convergence proof: ready/background state, terminal memory/performance
   limits, HUD phase, and monotonic progress for a typed view/policy epoch.
   A seventh policy owns aggregate scene-cost admission, bounded growth and
   overload backoff, the pass-wide remainder shared by every compact-index
   window, retained-cut recovery, the overload revision witness, and stable
   calibration-series scheduling.  Its one decision owns the three-sample
   minimum, bounded twelve-frame headroom series, admitted-work frame barrier,
   post-frame rescan, and terminal budget-limited classification.  The
   controller no longer carries parallel probe counters, remaining-frame,
   rescan, or budget-terminal latches, and the write-only refinement retry
   timestamp from earlier iterations has been removed.  BObolViewController
   executes those decisions but no longer reinterprets their latch contracts.
   An eighth allocation-free policy now owns camera-local pose/scale
   classification, scale-demand refresh across bounded coverage, quality-
   probe pending/active/presented state, resident-growth rearming, the
   100 ms/10 Hz quality contract, and quiet scale-epoch retirement.  The
   controller executes the returned one-level ceiling and retains the stable
   presentation snapshot; it no longer carries parallel zoom-demand latches.
   A ninth scalar quality policy owns renderer-independent pixel-error,
   point-proxy correction, and discrete PoP-ceiling calculations, including
   finite-input sanitation and current-cut-relative overload correction.
   Move the remaining stable-presentation snapshot/handoff decision behind a
   value policy before declaring coordinator authority complete.  That
   handoff should retain a reusable interactive quality cut when its completed
   frame already met the less demanding stable deadline.  OSMesa Lucy can,
   for example, present PoP 7 in roughly 39-40 ms during scale input but
   currently settles back to PoP 6 because stable and interactive throughput
   evidence are isolated; preserving this safe lower-bound proof would improve
   fidelity without weakening either FPS target.

2. Source/client ownership remains split.  The expensive detached realization
   execution belongs to libBObol, but the 21k-line libged draw adapter still
   owns progressive-provider orchestration, transactions, manifests, hierarchy
   integration, and client routing.  Finish the semantic draw-intent boundary
   and renderer-neutral GED endpoint so qged, MGED, and gsh are thin hosts of
   the same service.

3. Implementation concentration is still a correctness risk.  database_source,
   draw_obol, view_controller, and SoCADAssembly/executor units combine too many
   contracts.  Split them behind the already established indexed/SoA and pImpl
   boundaries; do not replace compact registries with per-occurrence objects or
   whole-scene traversals.

4. Renderer-tier equivalence still needs systematic proof.  Deadline safe
   points and coherent retry now cover every executor, but visibility, style,
   PoP-cut, culling, and primitive-count semantics still have multiple tier
   implementations.  Add packet-level conformance and GL-state sentinel tests
   before further executor cleanup.

5. Production qualification remains larger than the current green smoke
   matrix.  The final gate must include cold/partial/warm multi-gigabyte scenes,
   50k+ distinct assets, repeated-instance visibility turnover, adaptive BREP
   bands, hierarchy edit/erase/selection stress, memory ceilings, and p95/max
   input latency on both render backends.

6. Resident growth of one very large PoP asset is no longer a GPU upload
   ceiling or a correctness/interaction blocker.  The retained provider
   now reads only activation chunks in (resident, requested] after compaction,
   extends the existing immutable population, and atomically publishes the
   replacement while the old frame remains drawable.  Lucy level 10->11 fell
   from roughly 650 ms cache I/O plus 350 ms generation construction to about
   250-275 ms total and becomes visible during a held zoom gesture.  Exact-size
   down-trims also release the richer vector capacity instead of retaining it.

   Both the multi-part atlas and the single-large-part ordinary VBO route now
   consume a producer-certified process-local lineage.  Across matching
   immutable generations they keep the GPU prefix, upload only the CPU suffix,
   and use glCopyBufferSubData for device-local migration when capacity grows.
   Lucy GUI telemetry proves cumulative full uploads remain unchanged through
   an active 10->11->12 zoom while suffix/copy/reuse counters advance.

   Zoom residency is now independent of the calibrated draw allowance.  A
   missing pixel-demanded suffix may load under worker/resident byte admission
   while its result keeps the previously affordable presentation cut.  Only
   one growth task is active per asset occurrence; successive wheel levels
   coalesce and the newest demand is reconsidered after completion.  Each
   completed scale frame may then expose one richer population under a
   bounded 10 Hz quality-frame allowance without waiting for input to stop.
   A successfully presented quality ceiling survives later wheel epochs.  A
   deadline miss or frame over 100 ms lowers only the O(1), render-only
   prefix ceiling; worker publication and interaction never rewrite a richer
   retained occurrence back to the minimum cut.  Camera-stale suffix results
   rebase through immutable asset plus source/content identity, not through a
   camera stamp which continuous input can legitimately outrun.

   The real qged cold/warm Lucy matrix now proves this contract on both
   System GL and OSMesa: residency grows and a richer effective level is
   visible while the zoom gesture is still active.  System GL additionally
   proves cumulative full uploads remain flat while suffix upload, lineage
   reuse, and device-local copy counters grow.  Stable pauses compact the
   prefetched tail and a same-scale return restores at least the initial cut.

   Stable overload recovery now also starts from the currently displayed PoP
   population.  It reserves minimum coverage for all visible occurrences,
   then retains the richest already-active cuts which fit the reduced scene
   allowance in screen-priority order.  It no longer resets a level-7 asset to
   level 0 and visibly rebuilds upward.  Fixed widget/readback overhead on a
   tiny cut cannot poison faces-per-second calibration, and unchanged retained
   ordinary VBOs are reusable capacity witnesses on OSMesa even though that
   renderer does not use System GL's prepared multi-draw tier.  A camera/
   policy/current-budget witness permits one late calibrated-headroom rescan
   at each enlarged allowance, preventing both premature coarse convergence
   and impossible-next-level repaint loops.  That witness is now an explicit
   requested replay and appears in the convergence pending state.  It must be
   consumed or canceled before STABLE; an unrelated later selection, HUD, or
   checkpoint repaint cannot reopen LoD work after convergence.  Its
   centralized readiness gate is also revisited after stable-presentation and
   point-proxy barriers clear, since either barrier can consume the frame
   immediately following the terminal planning pass.

   A motion-time renderer ceiling can also defer a richer resident cut without
   changing any draw count.  Stable handoff now converts that annotation into
   a post-frame rescan: the first ceiling-free frame is still bounded, and the
   richer retained data is reconsidered immediately afterward instead of
   leaving an unwitnessed background latch or a permanently coarse cut.

   The coverage, convergence, scene-budget, view-demand, pressure, headroom,
   publication, and compaction contracts are now deterministic value policies
   beside the phase machine.  They allocate no memory, are trivially copyable,
   and have direct boundary tests.  Coverage windows publish their saturating
   visible/covered proof atomically only after a complete non-rescan pass, so
   a partial inventory delta cannot claim the final denominator.  Readiness,
   terminal
   limitations, public phase, and progress fraction are one scene-pointer-free
   convergence decision rather than another state machine reconstructed by HUD
   queries.  The scene-budget policy owns renderer-calibrated total allowance,
   pass consumption, reversible retained-cut recovery, and exact bounded
   growth/backoff tiers.  It also owns the stable probe population witness,
   bounded replay series, post-frame admission rescan, and performance-limited
   terminal latch; the controller no longer carries parallel allowance,
   recovery, or calibration fields.
   The view-demand policy similarly owns the zoom refresh and one-population
   quality-probe lifecycle, while the scalar quality policy owns its pixel-
   error, point-aggregation, and discrete progressive-ceiling math.  Full
   cold/warm Lucy GUI runs on System GL and OSMesa prove active-gesture
   refinement, independent resident suffix growth, post-zoom compaction,
   same-scale restoration, zero AABBs, terminal convergence, and zero
   coordinator invariant violations after the extraction.  Completed motion
   and resident-publication barriers now issue an explicit host wake for a
   pending bounded probe, preventing an already-true generic work latch from
   hiding a richer resident cut until quiet.
   Pressure first observed while automatic recovery is
   disabled is consumed once if recovery is subsequently enabled; it does not
   require an artificial falling/rising edge.  Headroom threshold comparisons
   use exact rational boundaries, avoiding floating-point admission ambiguity
   at precisely 5 percent capacity and 120 percent frame time.  Applied result
   batches retain a bounded adaptive timer until exactly one presentation frame
   is requested; first-useful and stream-idle results publish immediately, and
   the HUD cannot report ready until that frame completes.  Resident compaction
   likewise cannot begin merely because workers are idle: minimum mesh
   coverage, retained-cut admission, calibration/headroom, presentation
   handoff, and result publication must all be settled.  LoD-disabled views
   bypass those LoD-only gates, and a coverage prerequisite with no progress
   witness retires its request instead of leaving an impossible background
   latch.

   Publication still copies the prior contiguous CPU prefix into the new
   PartGeometry, so worker construction remains O(prefix + suffix), not
   O(suffix).  A future chunked or stable-address progressive CPU container can
   remove that final copy.  Memory admission must include old-generation,
   replacement/suffix, and transient GPU storage before starting the
   operation.  Authored corner-normal meshes intentionally retain the
   cumulative fallback until cached vertex-split identity makes their suffix
   append unambiguous.

The numbered priorities below retain the fuller rationale and constraints.
Some “current state” descriptions are the historical starting point for work
which the status above now marks partially or fully implemented.


Priority 1: Make the LoD state machine explicit
-----------------------------------------------

Current state:

* BObolViewController::Impl combines camera and view hosting with coverage,
  submission, refinement, calibration, convergence, stable-presentation,
  memory-compaction, and result-publication state.
* The documented FALLBACK, COVERAGE, INTERACTIVE, SETTLING, STABLE, and
  COMPACTING phases are implemented by combinations of numerous Boolean flags
  and counters rather than by an explicit state machine.
* Source, camera, policy, and inventory revisions are represented by a mixture
  of integer revisions, raw source pointers, strings, and signature vectors.

Recommended change:

* Extract a private BObolLodCoordinator from BObolViewController::Impl.
* Define an explicit phase enum and named transition methods.
* Introduce typed source, view, policy, and inventory epochs.
* Give the coordinator sole ownership of:
  - coverage scheduling;
  - view-aware LoD demand;
  - scene budget allocation;
  - interactive quality policy;
  - convergence proofs;
  - stable-state calibration;
  - resident-level compaction; and
  - LoD result admission/publication.
* Keep camera, render-manager, host callback, framebuffer, and general view
  integration responsibilities in BObolViewController.
* Record one explicit progress witness for each phase so liveness and
  convergence can be validated mechanically.

Performance constraints:

* The initial extraction must be field-for-field and behavior preserving.
* Preserve bounded cursors and GUI-thread work quanta.
* Preserve sparse delta publication and the O(1) stable retained replay paths.
* Do not replace indexed operations with whole-scene traversals.
* Do not introduce heap allocation, strings, or virtual calls in per-occurrence
  planning loops.


Priority 2: Split SoBRLDatabaseSource behind a thin facade
---------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

SoBRLDatabaseSource and database_source.cpp currently combine:

* database identity and ownership;
* detached database snapshots;
* cold and warm source realization;
* compact occurrence storage and streaming;
* path, source, and occurrence indices;
* visibility-frontier state;
* material, selection, and edit state;
* LoD request and result journals;
* cache interaction;
* SoCADAssembly synchronization;
* bounds, render, pick, measure, snap, and export operations.

The stable public BDatabaseSource header also exposes the implementation of the
thread-safe compact occurrence stream, including synchronization and container
details.

Recommended change:

Keep SoBRLDatabaseSource as a thin Coin node/facade and extract private
components with narrow contracts:

* CadSourceState
  - database/source identity;
  - revisions and content hashes;
  - source-level fields and semantic state.

* CompactOccurrenceRegistry
  - structure-of-arrays occurrence storage;
  - path/source/asset indices;
  - visibility and liveness masks;
  - sparse geometry/style/selection deltas;
  - conservative and realized bounds.

* DatabaseRealizer
  - detached snapshot consumption;
  - hierarchy and occurrence realization;
  - cold/warm cache realization;
  - progressive result streaming.

* CadPresentationBridge
  - translation of registry deltas into SoCADAssembly updates;
  - part and instance lifetime;
  - presentation revision tracking.

* Query adapters
  - bounds;
  - pick;
  - snap;
  - measure;
  - edit and export.

Move the compact stream implementation out of the stable public header and
expose only a narrow producer/consumer interface.

Performance constraints:

* Preserve the compact structure-of-arrays representation.
* Preserve sparse mutation journals and existing direct telemetry points.
* Preserve immutable shared geometry handoff.
* Do not add a per-occurrence object hierarchy.
* Do not change numeric occurrence IDs into strings or repeated path resolution.


Priority 3: Move progressive source realization out of libged
-------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

src/libged/draw_obol.cpp owns the progressive provider, detached realization
jobs, source items, worker threads, manifests, database imports, compact
occurrence streaming, adaptive publication batches, draw transactions, feature
overlays, hierarchy updates, and query integration.

This contradicts the intended ownership model:

* libged should own command parsing, semantic draw/erase transactions, and GED
  session policy.
* libBObol should own CAD source realization, progressive scheduling, cache
  population, and retained presentation.

Recommended change:

* Add a BObolSourceRealizationCoordinator in libBObol.
* Have libged submit semantic draw intent and immutable source/snapshot
  requests.
* Publish realization status and scene deltas through a narrow observer or
  polling contract.
* Split the remainder of draw_obol.cpp into:
  - transaction adapter;
  - hierarchy/frontier adapter;
  - feature and overlay adapter;
  - query/pick adapter.

Behavior that must be preserved:

* unordered worker completion;
* warm-manifest probing before expensive database opening/import;
* adaptive stream batch sizing;
* staged source mesh reuse;
* global and per-job working-set admission;
* bounded GUI-thread publication time;
* early aggregate extent followed by leaf extents and meshes;
* cancellation without exposing partially mutated Coin state.


Priority 4: Restore a renderer-neutral public GED boundary
----------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

The public GED view interface includes BObol/BDisplayEndpoint.h and exposes
bobol_display_endpoint and bobol_endpoint_property_value.  The installed
ged/draw_obol.h header also exports Obol-named framebuffer, presentation,
lighting, shading, faceplate, and group-erasure operations.

Recommended change:

* Define an opaque renderer-neutral ged_display_endpoint.
* Define renderer-neutral display property values.
* Provide generic semantic operations for:
  - presenting a view;
  - capturing a framebuffer;
  - lighting policy;
  - shading policy;
  - faceplate/HUD policy.
* Route group and subpath erasure through normal GED draw transactions and the
  visibility frontier rather than through public renderer-adapter calls.
* Make ged/draw_obol.h private, then remove it once qged, MGED, and gsh have
  migrated.

Client responsibility after this change:

* qged owns Qt window, input, and host scheduling policy.
* MGED owns its display-manager host and legacy interaction mapping.
* gsh owns its terminal/headless host policy.
* None of the clients owns progressive CAD realization or LoD transitions.


Priority 5: Make realization cancellation and teardown non-blocking
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

The libged deferred realization job destructor requests cancellation and then
synchronously joins its worker.  Cancellation is checked between operations,
but a database snapshot, import, or large mesh preparation may be expensive.
Destroying a view or GED session can therefore block on worker completion.

Recommended change:

* Move jobs to a libBObol-managed worker pool/service.
* Make endpoint/view teardown unregister its interest rather than synchronously
  owning the worker thread.
* Add cooperative cancellation checkpoints around snapshot, import, cache
  generation, and mesh preparation stages.
* Reap jobs after worker acknowledgement.
* Continue to restrict Coin node and field mutation to the owner/render thread.
* Do not detach a worker which holds unowned raw pointers.


Priority 6: Share renderer semantics while retaining specialized fast paths
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

CadRendererGL.cpp implements numerous rendering routes, including immediate,
fixed VBO, GLSL VBO, instanced, flat, indirect/atlas, software wireframe, and
OSMesa-specific paths.  Multiple paths independently interpret visibility,
tombstones, effective PoP cuts, style, culling, subpixel state, and primitive
counts.  This has a high risk of cross-tier visual divergence.

Recommended change:

* Compile CadFramePlan into a shared resolved draw-packet/range representation.
* Resolve the following once where doing so does not add O(N) replay work:
  - part and instance liveness;
  - visibility;
  - effective PoP cut;
  - subpixel policy;
  - style and material;
  - culling/orientation policy;
  - primitive ranges and counts.
* Split renderer tiers into separate private executors responsible only for
  binding resources and submitting resolved packets/ranges.
* Move shader sources and shader construction out of the main renderer file.
* Add cross-tier conformance tests for:
  - visible instance sets;
  - primitive counts;
  - PoP cuts;
  - shaded and wireframe image tolerance;
  - GL state before and after every renderer route and early return.

Performance constraints:

* Retain exact retained indirect replay.
* Retain instancing and atlas admission.
* Retain the flat OSMesa/software route.
* Do not force all tiers through a virtual per-occurrence submission layer.
* Do not re-expand sparse retained plans into full-frame CPU lists.


Priority 7: Harden LoD identities, requests, and result payloads
---------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

BObolLodRequest combines:

* database and asset identity;
* occurrence routing;
* view and policy revisions;
* projected demand;
* provider identity and parameters;
* bounds and source statistics.

BObolLodResult and BObolViewLodState payloads can simultaneously carry legacy
mesh data, progressive mesh data, prepared CAD geometry, proxy geometry,
status flags, and repeated binding metadata.  This permits invalid or ambiguous
states and makes stale-result validation difficult.

Recommended change:

* Introduce zero-cost typed wrappers for:
  - SourceEpoch;
  - ViewEpoch;
  - PolicyEpoch;
  - InventoryEpoch;
  - OccurrenceId;
  - AssetKey;
  - SourceRoutingId.
* Separate request identity, routing, view demand, and provider request data.
* Define one structured LodRequestKey.
* Define one LodBindingMetadata structure.
* Use an explicit tagged result payload:
  - Mesh;
  - Proxy;
  - Directory/manifest;
  - Error/cancelled.
* Replace string-built view/source signatures with typed snapshots and hashes,
  with complete equality checks where collision correctness matters.
* Resolve source routing IDs to source pointers only in an owner-thread result
  pump; do not retain raw source pointers as cross-pass identities.

Performance constraints:

* Typed IDs and epochs must be trivially copyable and allocation free.
* Avoid std::variant or similar abstractions in inner loops if benchmarking
  shows measurable cost; a compact tagged union is acceptable.
* Restrict serialized strings to caches, diagnostics, and external APIs.


Priority 8: Correct empty-part bounds semantics
----------------------------------------------

Current state:

SoCADAssembly::partGeometryBounds creates a unit cube at the origin when a
PartGeometry has no point, wire, or shaded geometry.  This arbitrary geometry
can corrupt aggregate bounds, autoview, picking, and early extent display.

Recommended change:

* Add an optional explicit conservative bound to PartGeometry.
* Use channel bounds when renderable channels exist.
* Use the explicit conservative bound for a non-renderable placeholder.
* Treat no channels and no explicit bound as an empty bound.
* Add tests for:
  - aggregate autoview;
  - empty and proxy parts;
  - source-level and leaf-level startup bounds;
  - pick exclusion for non-renderable empty parts.


Priority 9: Keep the Qt tree design and batch selection transactions
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

The Qt tree's whole-row highlighting is implemented correctly through model
roles, delegate painting, and branch-area painting.  Selection notifications
use indexed path and object lookups and notify only affected materialized rows.
This is a good large-hierarchy design and does not justify a custom tree widget.

QgTreeSelectionModel does, however, duplicate selection mutation, recompute,
draw synchronization, view notification, and model notification across its
selection overloads and several early-return paths.

Recommended change:

* Introduce one selection transaction/batch helper.
* Apply all path mutations in a QItemSelection first.
* Recompute GED selection metadata once.
* Synchronize the retained draw selection once.
* Emit one view update and one affected-row notification batch.
* Retain the existing path-hash and object-instance indices.
* Consider caching per-row selection roles by selection revision only if a
  paint profile shows the role query itself is significant.


Priority 10: Separate diagnostics and test harness code from qged main
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

A large portion of qged/main.cpp implements progressive drawing telemetry,
event sampling, JSON reporting, and direct inspection of libBObol and
SoCADAssembly internals.  This makes the production executable a white-box
test harness and increases coupling between clients and renderer internals.

Recommended change:

* Add a QgProgressiveDiagnosticsSnapshot or equivalent diagnostics service.
* Give its JSON report a versioned schema.
* Put event generation, sample collection, validation, and report writing in a
  test-support library or dedicated driver.
* Keep qged main responsible for option parsing, application construction, and
  launching the host.
* Make deep diagnostics opt-in and cheap when disabled.
* Split the large qged shell test into fixture discovery, interaction scripts,
  validators, and report aggregation.


Priority 11: Split SoCADAssembly's private implementation
---------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

SoCADAssemblyImpl owns parts, instances, indices, selection and visibility,
BVHs, progressive journals, plan caches, subpixel classification, and the
renderer.

Recommended private components:

* CadSceneDatabase
  - parts, instances, transforms, liveness, style.

* CadPlanCache
  - retained plan revisions, sparse invalidation, packet/range reuse.

* CadSubpixelClassifier
  - view-dependent subpixel grouping and promotion.

* CadPickingIndex
  - instance and part BVHs, pickable masks, query revisions.

Keep the existing SoCADAssembly public API and pImpl boundary while moving
private code into focused implementation files.


Priority 12: Document and test concurrency contracts
-----------------------------------------------------

Current state:

BObolLodService's global byte governor, worker pool, and per-resident locking
are structurally sound.  No confirmed lock inversion was found in the reviewed
paths, but the permitted lock nesting and callback rules are not sufficiently
centralized.

Recommended change:

* Document the service and resident lock order.
* State explicitly that callbacks are invoked without service/resident locks.
* State which fields are worker-owned, atomic, pump-owned, and render-owned.
* Add ThreadSanitizer coverage for native worker/service tests.
* Add deterministic cancellation, stale-result, and teardown tests.
* Add adversarial result ordering tests using a fake service and fake clock.


Priority 13: Formalize renderer configuration and GL-state validation
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Current state:

SoCADAssembly correctly treats itself as the raw-GL boundary: it synchronizes
Coin state, restores the raw GL state it borrows, invalidates
SoGLLazyElement's cache, and pops its local state.  Clients should not work
around this with clears or presentation timing behavior.

Recommended change:

* Preserve the node-level GL-state boundary.
* Add sentinel tests for every renderer route and early return.
* Cache OBOL_CAD_* environment/debug settings into a per-context or immutable
  configuration snapshot rather than repeatedly reading environment variables
  inside render paths.
* Keep user-facing lighting and shading policy semantic and renderer neutral.


Performance invariants
----------------------

The following mechanisms are architectural requirements and must not be
removed by cleanup work:

* Retained PoP3D buffers whose active cut changes without rebuilding geometry;
  resident growth should likewise append/upload a suffix rather than rebuild
  the complete prefix.
* Pixel-exact terminal semantics rather than unconditional full-mesh loading.
* View-aware active and resident LoD levels.
* Scene-wide triangle, line, draw-call, frame-time, and memory budgets.
* FPS-aware interactive coarsening with hysteresis.
* Starting movement from existing retained levels:
  - rotation and translation keep existing levels unless FPS requires
    coarsening;
  - zoom adjusts up or down from existing levels.
* Visibility and subpixel rejection before expensive realization/submission.
* Coarse aggregation for subpixel objects where it preserves useful visual
  information.
* Sparse occurrence journals and delta scene updates.
* Immutable shared_ptr<const PartGeometry> handoff from workers.
* Geometry reuse across repeated instances.
* Batching, instancing, indirect replay, and atlas submission.
* Bounded GUI-thread update quanta and responsive input processing.
* Global and per-task working-set governors.
* Cold-realization parallelism limited by memory as well as CPU availability.
* Warm-cache manifest and prepared-geometry reuse.
* Stable-state resident compaction for non-visible or over-detailed parts.
* Indexed hierarchy, path, selection, and highlighting operations.
* Owner-thread-only mutation of Coin nodes and fields.
* Direct renderer GL-state restoration at the SoCADAssembly node boundary.

OBB policy:

* OBB is an optional cheap visual fallback when it is already cached or cheap
  to compute.
* OBB is not a mandatory stage between AABB/leaf box and mesh.
* OBB computation must not delay time-to-first-useful visual or contend with
  mesh realization for scarce working-set memory.


Implementation sequence
-----------------------

Phase 0: Freeze a trustworthy baseline

* Complete a clean Ninja build.
* Record the exact source and Obol revisions, build options, drivers, Mesa
  version, cache state, and test fixtures.
* Capture cold and warm performance and visual artifacts.

Phase 1: Behavior-preserving structural extraction

Measurements and non-regression gates
-------------------------------------

Record at minimum:

* time to aggregate target extent;
* time to complete useful leaf-box coverage;
* time to first mesh;
* time to representative scene coverage;
* time to pixel-exact stable state;
* input-event latency, including p95 and maximum;
* GUI-thread progressive-update time;
* render-frame p50, p95, and maximum;
* moving and stable FPS;
* LoD task concurrency and CPU utilization;
* draw calls and selected renderer tier;
* active triangles/lines and resident PoP cuts;
* cache hit/miss and staged-source reuse;
* resident, active working-set, peak transient, and GPU memory;
* convergence phase and reason;
* terminal box/proxy count;
* visual comparison images.

Required behavioral gates:

* No terminal lingering boxes unless the source genuinely cannot realize.
* No box/mesh oscillation or stable-state fallback loop.
* No disappearing geometry during normal LoD transitions.
* No holes introduced into manifold PoP3D approximations.
* Shaded and wireframe modes obey the same view-aware PoP policy.
* Orthographic detail depends on projected size and visibility, not depth.
* Perspective detail accounts for depth through projected size.
* Rotation and translation retain current levels unless frame pressure requires
  coarsening.
* Zoom starts from retained levels and refines/coarsens incrementally.
* Non-visible geometry may compact under memory pressure and reload cleanly.
* Selection and hierarchy states remain correct while draw/erase/edit changes
  the frontier.
* GUI/console interaction remains responsive during realization and scene
  publication.
* System GL and OSMesa render equivalent scene content within defined visual
  tolerances.
* All GL state sentinels are unchanged after SoCADAssembly traversal.

Performance acceptance:

* Use repeated runs and distributions rather than one wall-clock sample.
* Require no statistically meaningful regression in the large-scene metrics.
* Investigate any repeatable regression approaching ten percent before merging,
  even when smaller unit benchmarks pass.
* Use perf recordings to distinguish realization, publication, traversal,
  driver, shader, memory, and GUI-thread costs.
* Keep deep diagnostics disabled during authoritative FPS measurements unless
  the diagnostic overhead is separately quantified.
